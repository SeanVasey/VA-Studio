# D-16 — Test-payment finalization and frozen grant input

Status: **Accepted in [PR #64](https://github.com/VASEYDEV/VASEYAUDIO/pull/64), 2026-09-26**, within Sean's continuous-development authorization. Keep payment confirmation, purchase finalization and fulfillment distinct. Use a separate disabled-by-default local/testing flag and an exact, explicitly installed test policy. Eligibility is strict `confirmed_at < original OrderAttempt.expires_at`. The confirmation timestamp records when this application observed and retained verified payment; it does not claim the provider's payment occurrence time. Finalization may happen later without moving that cutoff. This conservative development rule does not settle production U-08 timing or late-payment policy.

Revalidate the entire retained order and payment evidence before writes. One transaction consumes the attempt's inventory reservation and any promotion use, records shared-scope unique exclusive sales, creates exactly one immutable grant per original line, freezes encrypted render input, creates pending exact-asset entitlements and writes a pending outbox. The grant's effective time is finalization time. Purchased revisions, not current catalog or preview choices, determine the original commercial promise. Preserve earlier grants when exclusivity changes future availability. The retained verified rights declaration must still be the latest declaration for its track; a newer declaration blocks fresh finalization. Retain that observed declaration and scope-control evidence without applying broad current publication readiness to the frozen purchase.

An explicit eligibility, rights, asset or inventory safety failure after valid confirmed payment records `paid_exception` atomically, retains pending resources and creates no grant or entitlement. Its pending outbox supports later operator work; no alert delivery or exception-resolution worker is included. Malformed or corrupt frozen evidence returns `changed`; transient infrastructure/storage failures return `retry`. Neither outcome invents an irreversible business exception or releases capacity. Known unavailable assets or an actual digest mismatch can establish an asset exception; a temporarily unreadable storage path cannot.

Original encrypted preparation records and hashes remain unchanged. A narrow resource-disposition proof supports reconstruction of their original pending snapshot, followed by a separate complete finalization graph verifier. Retained reads and repeated finalization verify the payment, policy, terminal resources, exact grants/render inputs, expected entitlements, exclusive sales and outbox, rejecting missing or extra effects. Historical validation does not depend on current enablement or policy configuration.

The WP-04/WP-08 interface freezes original buyer identity, seller, assent, review hash, full selection/pricing/disclosure, inventory scope binding and explicit grant/payment/finalization identities and times. The buyer remains an unverified guest. Existing license `renderer_version` records historical review-HTML provenance; it does not select a buyer PDF renderer. Pending entitlement rows cannot authorize downloads. WP-08 must supply an offline deterministic PDF profile, durable original documents, guarded activation and authorized delivery, with an explicit extension of the current pending-only fulfillment graph.

An ID-only asynchronous finalization job is scheduled after payment commit; a bounded scoped console scanner recovers missed dispatch and advances past failures with a keyset cursor. Finalization performs no provider I/O. Customer owner-checked reads show verified payment separately from awaiting finalization, paid/pending contracts or paid exception/blocked fulfillment. Return URLs remain read-only and cannot create paid effects.

Migration rollback refuses populated finalization evidence or consumed resources before changing schema. Operational rollback disables new finalization and preserves all retained evidence, resources and encryption keys. Empty schema down/up belongs only to disposable databases; tests explicitly recreate their disposable database. No real Stripe transaction, refund, automatic release, PDF worker, active download, live enablement or cutover is included. See [the implementation contract](../test-payment-finalization.md) for exact policy, schema, states, commands and acceptance boundaries.
