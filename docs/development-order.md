### Production malware scanner fit — September 30

The media worker can use a resident `clamd` through `clamdscan --fdpass`, which [Media operations](media-processing.md#production-malware-scanner) now recommends for production; per-call `clamscan` stays the fallback. Before it trusts a daemon, every scan asks it about a sparse canary of 4 GiB and a byte and requires the MaxFileSize alert, so with a daemon left on the packaged, fail-open settings every scan fails as `scanner_unavailable` and the media waits for a retry, instead of passing, and `vasey:doctor` runs the same check as `media_scanner_limits`. A canary that would take real disk is refused. `clamscan`'s size limits are derived from the stems ceiling (1280M), and it alone gets 3 GiB of address space and 180 CPU seconds; the version call, the canary and the scan of one file share one 300-second wall limit. The 360-second stems budget now bounds every scan, FFprobe and FFmpeg call, so by the limits a stems run takes at most 690 seconds of subprocess time against the job's 900. Signatures are dated in UTC, and the worker log gives the reason for every scan that did not finish or was not confirmed clean. The integrating PR records the exact tested source, checks, real ClamAV 1.5.4 measurements and review.
