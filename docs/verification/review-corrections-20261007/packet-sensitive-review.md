## Atomic admission and retained proof

The old standalone historical lookup acquired and released the actor lock, returning absence before the separate creation transaction. Both callers could commit that absence. After one created the packet, the other still entered the fresh branch and refused its now-present selectors. This failed exact replay even though the request was unchanged.

The correction repeats the bounded creator/key lookup through the captured `CurrentRows` inside the capability adapter callback, after the core has acquired its actor row lock. On MySQL this is a direct captured-primary `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`, not an ordinary relationship or old consistent read. At most one matching row is admitted. A winner must match the canonical hash of the complete normalized signed request before `PacketEvidence::retained()` authenticates the encrypted canonical envelope, exact creator/key/request, source draft/candidate/approval and machine/context commitments, complete line set and sole packet audit. The returned model is constructed before final proof. Another confirmation of the same request may have a different public UUID; replay returns the winner's retained UUID and full row identity.

The historical branch performs no encryption, insert or new audit, and does not interpret the current catalog as new sale eligibility. Changed requests cannot recover an unrelated winner, and verification of the signed capture and actor identity still precedes both historical paths. Current Gate/staff/MFA checks and raw actor/audit stability remain in the core. Current authority is required for historical access; the previous capture authority hash is not incorrectly imposed as new authority over old evidence.

After callback-producing authority refreshes, the historical terminal branch compares the winner's actual id/public-id/creator-key selectors, full lines and audits, and raw source/capability family history against the already authenticated retained snapshot through the same captured primary. It reuses the pre-existing standalone historical proof without weakening it. The existing core source/family/user/audit proof then follows unchanged. The terminal guarantee consists of fixed PDO reads and pure consumer comparisons plus trusted standard crypto/pure helpers in the existing core proof. It does not sandbox arbitrary app-bound encryptors, arbitrary PHP, or transaction lifecycle callbacks. No Eloquent or `QueryExecuted` read follows the terminal consumer verifier under that trusted contract.

The absent branch's complete fresh body and `proveCurrent()` are byte-identical to baseline, as are `ReadProductionTrackCapabilities`, `ProductionTrackCapabilities`, `CurrentRows`, `PacketAuthority`, `PacketEvidence` and all migrations. Existing supported capability, actor authority, catalog baseline, graph/selector, licensing/media and final current-primary checks remain intact. The packet stays staff preparation only: execution/payable/external-facts/private-byte verification flags remain false, tax/total remain null, and no buyer assent, hold, order, grant, provider or live-operation authority is created.

The existing adapter still validates current candidate eligibility and context **before** invoking the new callback. A saved packet found by the initial standalone historical path remains recoverable after source successors, closure or catalog movement. If closure or source movement during the miss-to-adapter gap prevents admission to the unchanged core callback, that call can still refuse. This repair does not promise unconditional recovery across that separate condition or weaken source semantics to provide it.
