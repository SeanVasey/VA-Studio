# unresolved after a further 3 s. The page itself received the full JSON in every run.

edits of it); it imports playwright-core from the main checkout by absolute path.
Conclusion: on Chromium, Playwright cannot reliably observe completion or body of a response the page consumes through a
stream reader, whatever the routing. Page-side evidence is reliable.
