## 1. Batch inserted source closure into the mandatory committing boundary

Keep `MembershipReservations::proveInsertedActivation` and its Context implementation intact in substance. It must capture a **new real** complete member history and 257 scope; compare exactly original period/redemptions, original events plus the one planned consume, and original member graph plus the fixed plan/support; perform the full ledger/history audit; retire the old snapshot and adopt only that exact new snapshot/consume; admit the inserted state only once; and check original terminal/honor/freshness/authority/deadline constraints.

After that succeeds, the operation may replace `MemberActivationInspection::insertActivation`'s immediate full `proveCurrent()` with callback-free configuration/Budget/materialization/Rows/native-frame/finish admission. The inserted history's cryptographic authentication is callback-capable; this step does not pretend those callbacks were already followed by a full source proof. Instead, it keeps the graph privately provisional and the transaction uncommitted until the real final boundary below.

`MemberActivationObserver` must retain its full source `proveCurrent()` **after all ordinary TransactionCommitting delegates** and before physical commit. There must be no branch that marks `proved`, physically commits, supplies a grant/activation result, finishes a source frame or admits external authority based only on the inserted graph/admission. The existing fixed SourceCapture path proceeds directly from private insert completion to the owned normal commit, so this batching is coherent. Ordinary delegates already precede the final full proof and can themselves withdraw sources; the final closure must detect either that withdrawal or withdrawal during inserted-history authentication and roll back/refuse as appropriate.

Do not merge the exact inserted graph audit with a merely fresh graph, omit expected-row comparisons, reuse the old snapshot, remove original honor/cap checks, or replace the final observer proof with the earlier preinsert proof. Keep the distinct committed frame, complete persisted graph/source checks, one finish and postfinish checks. Preserve truthful committed-refusal classification if physical commit actually occurred; no compensating deletion/retry of a durable graph is introduced.

The meaningful regression for this refinement is withdrawal during actual inserted-history authentication, requiring final committing refusal with no retained effect/consume and idle handles. The existing ordinary committing withdrawal case covers a separate delegate mutation; retain it. Assertions should concern authority/state, not the count of private proof calls.

## 2. Add an exact one-use provisional artifact-plan permit

Create a **distinct** `MemberActivationArtifactPermit` identity/registry for the Sources completion owner. Its only issuance path must require the actual active, current `MemberActivationInspection` admitted through `MemberActivationCompletionPermit`, actual authorization proof/claim, the genuine materialization and original Budget. Review/confirmation, initial detached activation capture, ordinary C4b capture, caller-constructed permits and retired/foreign frames must not issue or consume this registration.

Bind the complete tuple: Sources/activation owner, concrete artifacts/facts/reservations/authorization services, exact inspection, exact materialization and source-values identity, exact intent and facts proof, principal/actor, Rows/captured PDO, original Budget/deadline and current phase. Content equality alone does not replace exact registration. Consume it once before admitting the requested pair, including on refusal, and remove remaining registrations in the owner's `finally`/retirement paths. No reused effect/completion/C4b permit, public boolean, generic caller-selected mode or first-arbitrary-consumer registration is allowed.

Use a separately named provisional artifact-plan method. It may check the exact permit/active inspection tuple, existing materialization claim/manifest/source equality, original physical observation/Budget and captured configuration/frame state, then register a private provisional receipt against the usual exact inspection/intent/facts/principal/actor/Rows/PDO tuple. It may do pure bounded manifest/value checks and captured-native admission, but must not manufacture a new physical observation, new Budget or source authority. Constructor completion still does not issue public readiness or confirmation authority.

Leave public/C4b `MemberOriginalArtifacts::admitReady`, `MemberOriginalSourceCapture::readyInspection` and `proveReadyCurrent` full. In particular, do not make a generic optional permit/flag skip `readyInspection::proveCurrent` for all callers. The new private registration supplies only the manifest/materialization needed for the operation's fixed planned rows. A directly invoked `prepare`/token/value factory still lacks the actual issuer registration.

The operation's mandatory preinsert `SealedMemberGrants::proveReadyCurrent -> proveGrantPlan -> MemberActivationInspection::proveCurrent` must remain after all record encryption/UUID/clock/history/audit callbacks. That combined closure must check exact authorization/facts/reservation sources, their terminal raw comparisons, complete plan/materialization registry/currentness, native frame/configuration and original physical/Budget/honor bounds before the planned-to-inserting transition and captured-PDO writes. The provisional artifact receipt cannot itself select writes or leave the operation as an operative readiness result.

Useful negatives for the new seam are shaped/replayed/foreign permit or C4b-frame refusal, and an actual source withdrawal during planned record construction that is caught by the retained preinsert closure. They must use genuine services/registrations; a passing fake or a pure DTO check cannot prove this boundary.
