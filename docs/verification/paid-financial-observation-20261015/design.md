## Required design clarifications before implementation

1. **Unattested input.** The caller-supplied GET-shaped array is not authenticated provider evidence. Even when all relationships agree with the actual original-paid expectation, normalization proves only input consistency. Name the internal value `FinancialCandidate`; its explicit summary uses `inputState=consistent_candidate`, `providerAuthenticated=false`, `inputProvenance=unattested_input` and never `observed`, `verified`, `clear`, `refunded` authority or settled balance. A future durable/provider layer needs authenticated retrieval evidence AND original source/positive-commit gates. This value alone cannot authorize dispatch, financial writes, grants, download holds, rights changes or inventory release.
2. **Exact summary.** The fixed summary is schemaVersion1/purpose`paid-financial-candidate-v1`, the above three literal input/provenance fields, originalSourceKind (`production_checkout_v1` or `production_tax_checkout_v2`), currency`USD`, originalGrossMinor, chargeAmountRefundedMinor, chargeRefundedFlag, chargeDisputedFlag, refundCountsByStatus, refundSumsMinorByStatus, refundSucceededSumMatchesChargeAggregate, disputeCount and disputeCountsByStatus. It carries no provider/account/buyer identifiers, raw source body, credentials, legal disposition or entitlement capability. Refund keys exactly pending/requires_action/succeeded/failed/canceled; dispute keys exactly warning_needs_response/warning_under_review/warning_closed/needs_response/under_review/won/lost/prevented, each including zero count. Charge.amount_refunded remains distinct from every status sum. Each refund amount is1..originalGross; succeeded sum cannot exceed gross; historical failed/canceled/pending attempt sums may exceed gross across distinct attempts and are preserved as integers, never totaled as settled. Lists100 and gross<=99,999,999 bound sums on admitted64-bit PHP. Succeeded sum need not equal the separately read Charge aggregate: retain the comparison boolean without upgrading it to atomic/final evidence. A contradictory Charge refunded flag/invalid bounds/changed before-after Charge or invalid subject/list/status is attention; has_more is incomplete. Dispute history count/status remains separate from the current disputed bit and may include closed/won/lost historical objects. No inference of an active dispute or legal outcome from history alone.
3. **Factory and lifecycle guard.** Both expectation factories and the normalizer admit the exact enabled/purpose/version/SDK/API literal configuration and local/testing environment before source extraction or normalization, with raw plain-map/core-repository/standard literal-env admission before offsets and no factory/object evaluation. Recheck that same captured policy after each callback-capable source proof/extraction and before returning a value; withdrawal cannot normalize or expose a tuple. Existing typed source methods still own their retained SQL/marker proofs in the caller's frame. These wrappers do not commit, roll back, release, replace or mint a source/receipt/frame. Expectation and candidate have private construction, clone refusal, __serialize and __unserialize refusal, JSON serialization refusal and bounded explicit non-authority summary only. Tests exercise both reconstruction directions and current flag withdrawal. A failing factory leaves cleanup to the actual caller that owns the frame.
