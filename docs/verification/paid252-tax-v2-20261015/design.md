## Original rendering and whole-order preparation

A V2 retained input/profile/version, Text builder, isolated renderer process and entrypoint display every original nested tax fact/full license/assent/source commitment. The approved base font/package/assets are referenced unchanged; a newly versioned manifest pins only the new implementation. Old V1 profile/runtime validation and pending/completed originals remain unchanged. No network/provider/decryption/config lookup occurs in the pure renderer child. Physical I/O is outside database transactions and bounded by an explicit existing lease/observation budget; no time limit is extended to obtain a green test.

Private work claims are durable, bounded and mutually exclusive, with immutable original manifest/profile/input/text/PDF hashes and exact path/size/provenance. Retry retains one logical origin/original per line; a failed or expired claim never overwrites an existing original. Claim wins and physical evidence are revalidated under original current authority and SourceV2 immediately before publication. Complete-order proof requires all original lines/documents and exact purchased bytes; partial/missing/damaged output activates nothing. Completed originals are served from retained bytes by a later reviewed delivery path, never regenerated from current catalog/customer/license/profile state.
