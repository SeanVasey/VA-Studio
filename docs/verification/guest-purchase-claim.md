# Explicit guest test-purchase saving

This bounded WP-08 increment lets the original guest browser save **one completed paid test purchase** to a currently authenticated test account. It is disabled by default and requires local/testing, `VASEY_TEST_CUSTOMER_ACCOUNTS_ENABLED=true` and `VASEY_TEST_PURCHASE_CLAIMS_ENABLED=true`. Production identity, lost-browser recovery, email-based matching and live commerce remain separate work.

## Customer flow

On customer sign-in, enter the exact guest test-order reference in **Bought as a guest?**, then choose **Select purchase before sign-in**. The server must recognize that order's original guest session and verify its paid finalization and issued original contracts. The browser supplies no owner key, account ID, bearer proof or buyer email to this operation.

A single pending proof remains in the private server session for ten minutes. Successful sign-in binds it to that first account, access version and credential stamp. The library presents the exact purchase and requires **Save purchase to this account**. Sign-in alone never saves anything. A deliberate exact retry may confirm the same completed claim while the original proof/current credentials remain valid; changed order, challenge, account or credentials cannot replay it.

The saved purchase appears in later valid sessions through account history, reference lookup, original item details and the existing original-file download flow. The original guest still has its original access. Account withdrawal, staff promotion, missing verification, credential changes and current feature withdrawal invalidate stale access. A fresh eligible login after credential recovery can read its already saved purchase.

This is deliberately not general guest recovery: signing in clears the original guest owner. If the pending proof expires after sign-in, this flow cannot reconstruct that lost guest session or infer ownership from an email address or reference. Signing out removes the pending proof.

## Evidence and authorization

- `customer_purchase_challenges` retains immutable original-order possession, original payload hash, hashed random proof, policy and exact 600-second interval. Four challenges per order per hour bound creation under the order mutex.
- `customer_purchase_claims` has unique order and challenge constraints and immutable encrypted evidence identifying the exact original order and target account. Every read reconstructs this binding against the current principal and unchanged original order; status, item, delivery and history projections recheck claim availability after graph reconstruction so an observed withdrawal cannot escape in a response. Challenge expiration limits completion; it does not expire an already saved purchase.
- The narrow `PurchaseAccess` resolver applies only to one order's history, status, original items and delivery. Quotes, checkout, sibling orders sharing a guest owner and other accounts retain their prior authorization boundaries. No `owner_key`, payment, finalization, grant, contract, entitlement or asset row is rewritten.
- Claim writes fence **user → account → order → challenge → existing claim**. Competing accounts serialize on the same order; withdrawal serializes on the customer user/account fences. Audit failure, commit-time expiry and gate/authority withdrawal roll back the entire new claim.
- Delivery continues to bind authorizations to the **original owner** and original grant/file hashes, retain the existing rolling budget and one-use/expiry rules, and freshly verify private bytes outside transactions. Account and exact claimed-order access are checked again after byte preparation and after audit, before commit.
- Both supported databases reject direct update, delete and replacement of possession/claim evidence. Canonical timestamps, foreign keys and unique bindings are guarded. Empty migration rollback validates exact owned table/column/index/foreign-key/trigger definitions and all incoming references before any DDL. Retained rows, foreign or changed objects, partial schema and temporary shadows refuse rollback unchanged. Legacy parent roundtrip tests remove and reinstall this additive empty child in dependency order.
