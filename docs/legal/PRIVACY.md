This policy explains what personal information VASEY.AUDIO collects when you visit the store, create an account, buy or download a license, or contact us; why we collect it; who we share it with; how long we keep it; and the choices and rights you have.

## 1. Who we are

The store at vasey.audio is operated by **Vasey Multimedia, doing business as VASEY.AUDIO** ("VASEY.AUDIO", "we", "us"). Vasey Multimedia is the controller of the personal information described here. The music sold in the store is produced and licensed by **Sean Vasey**, the store's owner and licensor.

- Postal address: [registered postal address — inserted at publication]
- Email for privacy questions and requests: **support@vasey.audio**

VASEY.AUDIO is a single-seller store. We sell only our own catalog; there are no other sellers, and we do not run a marketplace.

## 2. The short version

- We collect what we need to run the store, deliver your purchases and keep proof of your licenses: your account details, your orders and licenses, records of your downloads, and basic technical data such as your IP address.
- Card payments are taken on Stripe's secure checkout page. We never see or store your full card number.
- We do **not** sell your personal information, share it for cross-site advertising, or use analytics or advertising trackers.
- We email you about marketing only if you have told us you want that. Buying something never signs you up.
- Your license records are kept for as long as your license can be used, because they are your proof of license.
- You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete what we are not required to keep. Email support@vasey.audio.

### 3.4 Free downloads

Some tracks may be offered under a free license. To claim one you need an account, you type the name you want on the license, and you tick a box to accept its terms. We keep the name you entered, your acceptance, the exact terms you accepted, the license PDF and your download records, for the same reasons and on the same legal bases as a purchase (section 3.3). A free download never signs you up for marketing.

### 3.6 Embedded videos

Some pages include YouTube or Vimeo videos. **No video player loads until you press "Load video".** Before that, nothing is sent to YouTube or Vimeo. When you press it, the provider receives your IP address and may set its own cookies; we use YouTube's privacy-enhanced (`youtube-nocookie.com`) player and Vimeo's "do not track" setting. You can remove the player again with "Remove video player". YouTube's and Vimeo's own privacy policies apply to what they collect.

### 3.7 Information we do not collect

- We do not use analytics, advertising, tag-manager, session-replay or social-media tracking scripts.
- We do not load web fonts or scripts from third-party networks; they are served from our own server.
- We do not collect precise location. We do not try to work out where you are from your IP address.
- We do not ask for government ID, date of birth or other sensitive information.

## 6. Marketing email

- We send marketing email only if you say yes on your **Communication preferences** page, against the exact notice shown there. We record what notice you answered and when.
- Buying a license, claiming a free download, creating an account, contacting us or having your address in records imported from our previous store never counts as a yes. If we have not asked you, your preference is "unknown", and we treat unknown as **no**.

- Account, security and order messages are not marketing; we send them whatever your marketing choice is.

## 8. How we protect information

- Sensitive records (orders, license documents, payment notifications, contact messages, attachments, listening notes, consent records and delivery records) are encrypted in our database. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
- Purchased files, stems and license documents are kept in private storage that is never publicly reachable. Every download needs a valid license and a fresh, short-lived authorization, and each authorization works once.
- The site is served over HTTPS. Account and order pages tell browsers and search engines not to cache or index them.
- Staff sign in to the store administration with multi-factor authentication, and staff access to customer records is logged.
- Uploaded files are scanned for malware before anyone can open them.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you and the authorities where the law requires.

## 10. Your rights

### 10.1 Everyone

Whatever your location, you can ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct inaccurate information;
- delete information we are not required to keep;
- give you your listening library in a portable format (you can also export it yourself);
- stop marketing email (see section 6).

Some records cannot be deleted while they are still needed: your orders and license documents are your proof of license and our tax records, so we keep them for the period in section 7 even if you close your account. We will tell you if this applies.

### 10.2 EEA, UK and Switzerland

You also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You can complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office), although we would appreciate the chance to help first.

### 10.3 California and other U.S. states

California residents (and residents of other U.S. states with similar laws) have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to access, correct and delete it; to opt out of its sale or sharing (we do neither); and to limit use of sensitive personal information (we use your account login only to sign you in, which the law permits). We will not treat you differently for using these rights. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of the agent's authority and verify your identity directly.
